# Purpose

Every value in a signature must have a criterion verifiable in text: not an
abstract definition but a question answered yes/no against observable
evidence. Arbitrariness is reduced by triangulation with the already-diagnosed
corpus (the Anchor Set) — never by forced quantification. The accumulation
mechanism is interpretive jurisprudence (validated precedents), not weight
retraining.

# 1 · Todorov axes — procedure

For each axis the analyst applies the activation questions in ascending order,
with cited evidence, and proposes **the highest value with an affirmative
answer sustained in at least two independent sources**.

**Dissociation rule.** The analyst must not assume correlation between axes.
px can be low while ax is high (the elite-Other case); ep can reach 5★ while
px stays moderate. Dissociation is diagnostic — it is information, not noise.

## 1.1 Praxeological axis px — the thermometer (full rubric)

| px | Boundary descriptor | Activation question |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Assimilation: the Other can become Us | Is there an institutional path to full rights through integration? |
| **2** | Soft segregation: separated but tolerated | Is there systematic separation without expulsion or elimination? |
| **3** | Expulsion or physical/legal confinement | Is there deportation, internment, or active de-inscription? |
| **4** | Systematic violence with structural impunity | Is physical violence institutionalised, not episodic? |
| **5** | Physical elimination of the group as such | Is there an elimination policy with planning or mass outcome? |

Corpus finding: the economic limit brakes px below 5 — where the Other retains
functional utility, treatment stops short of elimination (τ₂ family).

## 1.2 Axiological (ax) and epistemological (ep) axes — endpoint anchors

The canon fixes verified endpoint anchors for ax and ep; intermediate values
are read from the case diagnosis against Anchor Set precedents, per the
governing rule (*if it cannot be read from an existing diagnosis, it does not
enter the notation*):

| Axis | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **ax** | The Other valued as equal | The Other as existential threat |
| **ep** | The Other known through its own voice | **5★** — erasure of the Other's own knowledge |

The **5★** marker names the level at which resistance takes generations to
rebuild, because the Other's own epistemic resources have been destroyed, not
merely silenced. Full intermediate boundary descriptors for ax and ep are a
declared expansion for a later codebook version; until then, coding of
intermediate values must cite the Anchor Set precedent on which it leans.

# 2 · Node IV — the ten modes of obedience (nominal typology)

Unlike Todorov, obedience is **not** an ordinal scale but a nominal typology:
several modes coexist in a case; one dominates. Detection in three steps:
binary presence per mode → identification of the dominant by causal argument →
mapping of the characteristic fracture.

| Dominant mode | Characteristic fracture | Paradigmatic case |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Interiorised | Legitimacy crisis that breaks the naturalisation | All cases |
| 2 · Performative | Coordination of private disobedience | Apartheid, Rohingya |
| 3 · Strategic | Cost of collaborating exceeds cost of resisting | Spain 1492, Jim Crow |
| 5 · Without legal subject | Minimal legal recognition (Node I) | Rohingya |
| 6 · Bureaucratic-routinised | Individual responsibility made explicit | Nazi |
| 7 · By identitarian choice | Fracture or change of the identity | AfD/Lukreta |
| 8 · Gradual anti-institutional | Non-capturable counterweights | MAGA |
| 9 · Institutional fracture | Expansion of non-compliant actors | EU, MAGA |
| 10 · Direct material dependence | Exhaustion or diversification of the resource | MORENA |

Numbering reaches ten although the table lists nine rows: **mode 4
(resistant)** describes active disobedience and is catalogued apart, because
it is not a mode of obedience but its negation.

**Reading of the full bridge.** Pegoraro & Rinella's five sources answer *why*
a norm binds; the ten SOCE modes answer *how* it is obeyed and *where* that
mode breaks. Each mode's fracture is the design input for intervention — the
specific contribution of Node IV.

# 3 · Node II — A_core texture

The decisive element of the ideological anatomy is the **A_core**: the set of
claims believers cannot give up without abandoning the ideology; the remaining
elements exist to protect it. The core is graded by how open it is to being
proven wrong — from theological (essentially unfalsifiable) to
securitarian-administrative (easily checked) — with the working rule: **the
easier a core is to disprove, the more external support it needs to survive**,
and the more the case depends on Node III's technology of imagination
(transmission II.A_core → III). A_core texture also conditions the dominant
obedience mode (personalist → identitarian; theological → interiorised;
bureaucratic → routinised).

# 4 · What the rubrics do not eliminate

Being explicit about residual arbitrariness is part of the rigour. Genuinely
ambiguous readings are not forced to a value: they carry an explicit
**uncertainty flag (?)** and enter the validation queue, where the human
analyst audits them (see `protocol/extraction_protocol`). Ambiguity is made
traceable, not eliminated.
