# SOCE — Social-Ontological Cycle of Exclusion

A five-node diagnostic architecture for reading how contemporary democracies
naturalise the exclusion of the Other.

Part of the [Computational Ontology](https://computational-ontology.com)
research programme — *the ontological systematization of social, real and
ideal objects*.

| Path | Contents |

| `anchors/` | Worked anchor cases fixing the meaning of each ordinal state |

## How to use this — and how not to

SOCE produces a **structural signature** Φ = ⟨σ, V, E⟩ read from documented
inscriptions: node intensities as ordinal states, the three Todorov axes, and
the states of the inter-node links.

**Two-tier use rule.**

- **Exploratory use is open to all.** Load the codebook, read a case, produce a
  provisional reading. Any analyst — human or machine — may do this.
- **Canonical diagnosis requires two trained coders under the
  delta-protocol.** A signature produced without that validation is not a
  canonical SOCE signature and must not be presented as one.

This is not gatekeeping for its own sake. Reproducibility here is defined as
*two independent analysts producing the same signature* — not as a statistical
coefficient. A single unvalidated reading, however fluent, does not meet the
definition.

## Notation constraints

The canon is qualitative-ordinal. Ordinal states are never averaged, summed or
weighted, and no continuous quantification enters the notation. These are not
stylistic preferences: averaging ordinal states across autonomous analytical
frameworks would treat them as components of a single measurement instrument,
which they are not.
